MDK Sensor

Moto Mods DeveloperLibraries & Demo
Everyone
6

MDK Sensor is a simple app that provides an end-to-end example demonstrating how to obtain readings from the Sensor Personality card attached to your Reference Moto Mod, which it then displays in the app as graph.

For a detailed walk-through and details for this app, visit http://developer.motorola.com/build/examples/sensor

This app requires a Moto Z, Reference Moto Mod, and Sensor Personality Card. To purchase hardware, visit http://developer.motorola.com/buy

Note: This app is for development purposes.
